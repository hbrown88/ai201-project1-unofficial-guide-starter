Workshops

All workshops listed are posted in our CareerBuzz system.    By clicking on the event title, you will be taken to the event in CareerBuzz, where you have access to more information about the event and can express your interest in attending.   NOTE:  You must have an active CareerBuzz account to access this information.  And you can click on the Details link below the workshop to learn more about each particular event.   Any questions about any of these events can be sent to careereducation@gatech.edu.
