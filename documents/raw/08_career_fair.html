GEORGIA TECH CAREER FAIRS

Georgia Tech hosts a multitude of Career Fairs through different departments. This website is intended to showcase each of these fairs, but please note each department uses different virtual platforms and registration services. Please contact our Employer Connections Team at employerconnections@career.gatech.edu for general fair questions.

NOTE: ALL Georgia Tech Career Center fairs are open to students and alumni.
